Measured 2026-09-05 while hunting an ad niche. The categories that make the most money in iOS
Utilities **barely advertise on Meta at all**:

| category | top-40 grossing apps | total Meta ads (all media, all time) |
|---|---|---|
| Authenticator | 2 | **~5** |
| Universal TV Remote | **6** | ~129 active / **36 lifetime**, mostly burners, 0 install CTAs |
| QR scanner | 2 | ~240 |
| Cleaners | 7 | ~2,200 — **96% video**, carpet-bombed by burner pages |

**EVOLLY.APP** (TV Remote, 606,340 ratings, ~$1M/mo per Sensor Tower): **zero Meta ads ever**;
~400 Google ads that are **100% video, 0 image** and drive their *Android* app; iOS comes from
ASO + almost certainly Apple Search Ads.

**Why:** ~$1M ÷ ~600k downloads ≈ **$1.67 revenue per download** (~$1.42 net) against **US Meta
CPI $2–6**. Even the category leader, at 1.75x our price, cannot profitably buy US Meta installs.
Every USD advertiser who tried in that category switched off within 2–3 weeks; only VND
cheap-geo arbitrage persisted.

**Consequences.** (1) A utility is a *search* product — the need exists only at the moment of
need, which Meta cannot target and the App Store can. (2) **Apple Search Ads has no ad creative**
— it serves your listing, so static-vs-video is irrelevant there; Custom Product Pages (35 per
app) are the only creative lever. (3) Our own monetisation is the binding constraint, not the
channel: $3.99/wk + a dismissible paywall yields ~$0.20–0.50 per install, too low to buy almost
any traffic. Price and paywall hardness must move before any paid channel clears.
