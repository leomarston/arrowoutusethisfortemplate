## Genre playbook (GAMEPROMPT §10.9, verbatim) + adaptations
### 10.9 Escape / unblock / parking (Arrows, Tap Away, Unblock Me, Parking Jam)
- **Rules to pin:**
  - escape: a tapped piece leaves along its path if its ray is clear, otherwise it bumps and costs a heart;
  - sliding: drag along the piece's axis; stars for minimum moves;
  - parking: a swipe moves a car forward or back, and a free car drives the loop to the exit;
  - the exact blocking rule; penalties; zoom.
- **Measure:**
  - the grid pitch, from line positions;
  - path geometry: stroke, corner radius, the head;
  - exit T(d) and acceleration; trail dots;
  - the bump-and-return: distance, T, colour;
  - hit tolerance; zoom and pan limits; hearts;
  - parking: the loop path, speed, the exit queue.
- **Engine:** Core Animation for flat puzzles (Arrows); SpriteKit for sprite cars; RealityKit, or 8–16 pre-rendered headings, for
  a 3D lot.
- **Art:** vector paths with no bitmaps; cars and blocks from the SDF pipeline.
- **Content:**
  - extract recorded levels exactly with overlay verification (`extract_levels.py`, IoU ≥ 0.99);
  - solve escape greedily (`solve_levels.py`), sliding and parking by BFS;
  - generate by adding pieces in reverse;
  - difficulty = dependency depth.
- **Meta:**
  - offline: hearts, coins, hints, skins;
  - locked: leaderboards.
- **Research:** extract → solve → play the solution; one deliberate mistake per level to measure the bump.
- **Spikes and V1:**
  - tech: 200 pieces under zoom;
  - V1: the recorded levels + generated levels to 100+.

**Adaptations for Maze Out (from the store page + first look; verify on the phone):**
- Sub-type: escape (tap), like Arrows, but TIMED ("plenty of moves, limited time"), with 3 hearts per level shown in the timer pill
  (store shots: 2:00 / 1:45 / 1:30 / 0:45 "Hard Level"). A wrong tap = bump + heart loss (verify); timer 0 or 0 hearts = fail (verify).
- Boards are big mazes of thin black snake arrows on white, often in a silhouette (heart, crescent), up to ~40×40 cells ("Hard Level").
  An exiting arrow leaves a RAINBOW trail (store shot 1 and 5). Measure the trail (colours, width, fade).
- Obstacles to pin (store copy "linked arrows, corners, pipes and much more"; store shots): locked PURPLE CRATES (hexagon lock) with KEY
  arrows (a key on an arrow opens a crate?), BLUE PIPES along the border with numbered caps ("2"), coloured borders (magenta/blue
  outline around a silhouette). Log every obstacle with its first level and exact rule.
- Meta seen: home (big illustrated factory scene: purple scientist monster at a console, arrow-filled capsule machine, two yellow worker
  characters, LEVEL N pill, big green Play), top bar (avatar, coins +, lives + timer/"Full", settings gear), bottom nav (shop basket,
  Home, trophy), event badges (Streak Race x1/x5/x10/x25/x100 with a leaderboard, Claw Challenge). Online (Streak Race leaderboard vs
  others?) → verify if it's real or bots; locked if online.
- Monetisation: coins, lives, shop; ad-free per the store. Reject every purchase (owner's order).
- Zoom: does the original pinch-zoom the big boards? Verify at kickoff; if yes, extend the runner with /pinch (§3.2).
- Engine (DECISION pending tech spike): Core Animation CAShapeLayer arrows as in apps/arrows (vector, no bitmaps on the board) + SwiftUI
  shell; home scene = illustrated art (SVG → @3x, or 3D pre-render via mfrender) with our own characters as cut-out puppets.
- Research minimum (§14): ≥ 30 levels on the phone. Here: L32 onward in session 1; L1–31 + FTUE after a reinstall, or from videos.
- V1 scope: every recorded level exact (overlay-verified, solver-proved) + generated levels in the original's curve to 100+; every obstacle
